## About

I’m Advaith, a Bengaluru-based software engineer and founder working where applied AI, quantitative systems, scientific computing, and product engineering meet. I build compact, observable systems that turn ambitious research ideas into software people can actually use.

Right now, that means building the software and research stack for Hydra Fund, along with open-source tools for grounded agents, molecular engineering, and quantitative research. I care about deterministic behavior, honest evaluation, and interfaces that make complex systems feel clear.

## Selected work

### Hydra Fund

Software and research infrastructure for an early-stage AI-native investment and venture platform.

### [zeroH](https://github.com/cosmic-hydra/zeroH)

A standard-library-first Python layer for grounded memory, retrieval, claim verification, citations, and abstention.

### [ZANE](https://github.com/cosmic-hydra/zane)

An experimental pharmaceutical research platform connecting molecular data, model workflows, safety gates, and laboratory interfaces.

### [Tetra Quant](https://github.com/cosmic-hydra/tetra-quant)

Quantitative-trading research infrastructure built on the OpenBB platform.
